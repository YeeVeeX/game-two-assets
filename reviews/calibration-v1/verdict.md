   The creature is unmistakably the same entity in all ten cells of each

## Structured critique and cross-vendor review (Kimi K2.5, adversarial)

First-round council verdict was **REJECT** with four attacks. Reconciliation
against the artifacts (models see text, not pixels — every claim re-verified
by measurement before acceptance):

1. "44.4% is the wrong ceiling; show popping is perceptually safe" —
   PARTIALLY ACCEPTED: the ceiling is necessary-not-sufficient; added the
   region-confinement analysis (step-pair deltas 100% leg-band, 0% head)
   as the sufficiency evidence.
2. "Down gait is subthreshold: 3 dark near-floor pixels, asymmetric beat
   9.43/9.43/1.2/1.2" — **CONFIRMED BY MEASUREMENT** (the review's real
   catch). Fixed in-scope: 1px planted-foot weight-shift sway on both down
   contact frames; beat now 19.64/19.0/15.87/15.87 with ±0.95px bright-mass
   centroid oscillation. This is the only pixel change the critique drove.
3. "Right cycle = disconnected rubber-band legs" — REFUTED with pixels: legs
   join the torso bottom row in every frame (4x inspection; spec rows);
   the claimed "industry 15-20% threshold" is uncited and inconsistent with
   its own rejection of 22% as too high.
4. "0,+1,0,0 bob may read as hitch" — UNCERTAIN then resolved by the sway
   revision (transition costs now near-symmetric).

Second-round council on the revision: claims 1 and 2 **CONFIRMED**, overall
**PASS** — "communicates step through mass redistribution rather than slide
through minimal floor-pixel churn; readably distinct from idle translation
at 1x." Council budget spent: 2,928 tokens of the 8k cap, two calls, one
consolidated final verdict.

## Decision

**PASS — the lane-B walk cycles are banked as the motion answer: the compact
silhouette holds identity frame-to-frame at native 1x. The unstable-blob
hypothesis is answered NO.** Animated motion is a genuine improvement over
sliding the static idle, confirmed by measurement, native-scale inspection,
and independent cross-vendor review after one critique-driven revision.

Status per contract: **selectable candidate only.** No integration —
game-two v17 remains open; every runtime-integration stop condition in
`docs/asset-contract.md` still applies. The optional 2-frame idle bob was
declined: the walk verdict is clean, and an idle bob tests a different
hypothesis (idle liveliness) — out of this sprint's question.

## Findings for any future integration design (recorded, no action now)

- **Grid phase lock** (council finding, held): 4 frames × 8px = exactly one
  tile, so the same frame always lands on tile boundaries if the engine maps
  frame index to slide phase 1:1. The 8px/frame slide on this sheet is a
  review convention, not an engine contract — the engine should choose frame
  timing independently of tile phase (or accept the lock deliberately).
- The v0 ring/ally-dim finding stands: the renderer rings the 28x28 SIZE
  constant, oversized for a 16-22px-wide body; ring/dim the sprite bbox at
  integration time.
- Walk↔stand is seamless by construction (the cycle passes through the idle
  as f3); an integration design can hold f3 as the stand pose.
