## Findings for any future integration design (recorded, no action now)

- The engine draws the possession ring and ally-dim as filled rects sized to
  the 28x28 `SIZE` constant. Under a 16x24-mass sprite the ring becomes a
  white backdrop plate (over-dominant) and ally-dim would dim floor pixels
  around the body. An integration design must ring/dim the sprite bbox or
  draw outlines instead. Found by the RING rows of this sheet.
- Lunge/telegraph swell geometry also assumes the full-square body; verify
  tells against lane B's silhouette during any integration replay capture.
